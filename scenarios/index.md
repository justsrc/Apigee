## Using these

Give the agent the initial ticket and initial evidence only. Hold back the
diagnostic evidence until after the first response, and the ground truth entirely.
A scenario that leaks its answer is not testing anything.

Score with `evaluation/rubric.md`, and with the scenario's own rubric table where
the two differ.
