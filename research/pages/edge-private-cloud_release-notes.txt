### What is a feed reader?

Really simple syndication (RSS) feed readers aggregate content from websites that you specify.

Feed reader notifications can be email-, browser-, desktop-, or mobile-based. Some readers are free, or have free versions, and some require a subscription.

A few examples:

- <a href="https://feedly.com" class="external" target="_blank">Feedly</a>
- <a href="https://feedreader.com" class="external" target="_blank">Feedreader</a>
- <a href="https://feeder.co/" class="external" target="_blank">Feeder</a>

More information on RSS:

- <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSS" class="external" target="_blank">RSS</a>
- <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comparison_of_feed_aggregators" class="external" target="_blank">Comparison of feed aggregators</a>
