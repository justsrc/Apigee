### comment by dangrasso (association=NONE) at 2025-06-26T07:37:48Z
Actually I know why it still fails @DinoChiesa @ssvaidyanathan I see that this replacement still happens a few lines above: https://github.com/DinoChiesa/apigeelint/blob/bc113b92f6f39ae7868f27f8b6f4480c32898514/lib/package/TruthTable.js#L484

The * quantifier will match on 0 or more occurrences of the space, so any condition starting with a variable starting with not... will be affected.

You probably want to match only on `^ *NOT +` to ensure there is a space after the not.
