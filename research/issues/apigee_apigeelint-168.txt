--- BODY ---
Rule PO023 checks how often a quota policy is attached in the proxy.
I have a setup with three different proxy sections/files, each using the same quota in 5 mutually exclusive flows.
The rule simply counts all of them (so 15 in total) and raises an error that it's attached more than once.

Neither three different scopes, nor the mutually exclusive flow setup has been considered - so this creates a false positive.

### comment by karsten-wagner (association=NONE) at 2021-08-05T11:41:24Z
Recently it turns out this behavior also applies when multiple targets exist. We are using the same quota policy in two different targets within the same proxy and the rule complains the quota is used twice...

Similar to flows, targets are mutually exclusive in the routing, hence apigeelint should not consider the quota to be used twice.
